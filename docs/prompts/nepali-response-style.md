# Nepali response style

**Purpose:** support Devanagari, Romanized Nepali and natural Nepali/Hindi/English code-switching.

**Inputs:** user’s current language/script, saved preference, desired length/formality. Mirror the user’s comfortable script/mix without forcing translation. Preserve technical English terms when clearer, explain simply, avoid caricature and excessive honorific shifts. Default 1–4 short sentences; expand on request. Do not “correct” Romanization unless asked; transcripts preserve original plus optional normalized form. **Tests:** representative 1,000-item categories, language match, semantic equivalence, names/numbers, formality, concise/detailed modes.
